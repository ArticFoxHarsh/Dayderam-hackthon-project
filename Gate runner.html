How to Play

Objective: Reach the final green gate to complete the level.

Controls: Use ←→ to run and ↑ or Spacebar to jump.

Health: You lose HP from bullets (-20), bombs (-30), orange gates (-20), and standing still for 1 second (-20).

Power-Up: After passing an orange gate, press ↓ to teleport past the next one. This is a sacrifice that sets your HP to 30. Using it with less than 30 HP is fatal!
